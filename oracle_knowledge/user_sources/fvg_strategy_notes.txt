## Usable FVG Rules

1. FVG is a three-candle imbalance: bullish FVG exists when candle 1 high is below candle 3 low; bearish FVG exists when candle 1 low is above candle 3 high.
2. The middle candle should be a strong displacement candle. Tiny gaps without impulse should be filtered.
3. FVG is not an entry by itself. It is a zone or directional clue; entry should require retest/rejection/BOS/CHoCH/engulfing or a momentum continuation rule.
4. Break of structure before or during FVG formation makes the FVG higher quality.
5. Liquidity sweep before FVG or before retest improves quality.
6. Unmitigated FVG has priority. Once price has already tested/filled a FVG, do not reuse it unless using a specific retest mode.
7. Candle reaction inside FVG matters: valid long reaction should close inside the FVG or back above it, not close below and invalidate it.
8. Higher-timeframe FVG can define the reaction zone; lower-timeframe FVG can define entry timing.
9. Entry timeframe should be meaningfully lower than HTF. For our option charts, practical pairs are 5m zone -> 1m/3m entry, or 15m zone -> 3m/5m entry.
10. Premium/discount filter: for longs, prefer bullish FVGs below the 50% midpoint of the impulse range. For shorts, prefer bearish FVGs above 50%. Since we buy options only, use this as a quality filter on the option premium chart.
11. Extreme FVGs have priority: lowest bullish FVG in a bullish impulse is stronger than upper bullish FVGs; highest bearish FVG in bearish impulse is stronger.
12. Support/demand confluence inside a bullish FVG improves quality.
13. Resistance/supply confluence inside bearish FVG improves quality.
14. If there are multiple FVGs, prioritize the one with best location, unmitigated status, structure break, target room, and support/demand overlap.
15. Inside-bar exception: if candle 1 is inside a mother candle, use the mother candle/four-candle sequence to avoid marking a wrong FVG zone.
16. News or very sudden event gaps can be dangerous because slippage is high; reduce size or avoid around major scheduled news.

## Entry Models For Our FVG Strategy

1. OR Break + FVG Retest: mark first candle range, wait for breakout through range with FVG, then buy retest/reclaim.
2. Conservative FVG Retest: bullish FVG forms after BOS, price retests gap, candle closes inside/above gap, then buy.
3. Engulfing Retest Entry: price taps FVG and the retest candle body is engulfed by bullish candle; buy after engulf confirmation.
4. Limit Entry Mode: buy at FVG start/top/mid when price returns, only if FVG is unmitigated and structure is valid.
5. HTF FVG + LTF FVG: HTF FVG is the area of interest; after price enters it, LTF must show sweep/CHoCH and create a new FVG for entry.
6. Breakaway FVG Momentum: after strong breakout and displacement FVG, buy continuation if next candle breaks displacement high and FVG remains unfilled.
7. Demand + FVG Model: FVG predicts retracement, but actual entry can be at demand candle before the impulse, especially if price reaches discount.

## Stop And Target Logic

1. Stop below FVG low/bottom for long.
2. Safer stop below displacement candle low or demand candle low.
3. If FVG is tiny, use ATR/minimum buffer to avoid normal noise.
4. If FVG is too large, enter at midpoint or use smaller internal zone to improve risk-reward.
5. Fixed targets from videos: 2R for slower/day-trade style, 3R for faster/scalping style.
6. Practical option target: 1:2 partial or breakeven, then next liquidity/recent high/HTF key level.
7. Target room filter is mandatory: avoid FVG buys if nearby resistance/liquidity is too close.
8. Trail only after strong continuation; possible trail levels are new bullish FVG bottom, swing low, or structure break exit.

## Filters For Indian Option Buying

1. Long-only on CE/PE premium chart.
2. Session filter: avoid first chaotic minute; practical start 9:20 or after first 5m range is formed.
3. Avoid late entries after 15:10-15:15 because decay/spread/slippage increases.
4. Use minimum FVG size and minimum displacement body filter.
5. Volume multiplier filter can help options: displacement candle volume above recent average.
6. One trade per FVG by default.
7. Avoid already-filled FVGs by default.
8. Avoid chop: too many small overlapping FVGs, no clean impulse, no structure break.
9. Keep FVG Mode options: Off, Displacement FVG, Retest FVG, Displacement Or Retest.
10. Keep entry mode options: Retest Close, Wick Touch, Midpoint Limit, Engulf Confirmation, Breakaway Continuation.

## What Not To Do

1. Do not buy every FVG touch.
2. Do not use FVG without displacement.
3. Do not reuse old mitigated FVGs repeatedly.
4. Do not buy upper/premium bullish FVGs when reward room is poor.
5. Do not ignore structure: FVG after no BOS/CHoCH is weaker.
6. Do not overfit with too many mandatory filters at once; start with a clean core and add toggles.
