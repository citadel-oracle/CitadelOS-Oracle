## Video 1

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2u9oYfx5xdY

Title: Best Order Block Trading Strategy You Can Find!

Channel: Smart Risk

Core model: order block within order block.

### Main Idea

Use a higher timeframe order block as the main trading zone, then wait for lower timeframe market-structure confirmation before entering from a lower timeframe order block.

Direct touch of a higher timeframe OB is not enough.

### Definitions

Order block:
- Candle before a fair value gap / imbalance.
- Candle color does not matter.
- The full candle can be used as the zone.
- If a liquidity-grab wick appears before the imbalance, include that wick in the zone.

Bullish order block:
- Candle before bullish FVG.
- Expected demand zone.

Bearish order block:
- Candle before bearish FVG.
- Expected supply zone.

Fair value gap:
- Fast move leaving imbalance.
- Used to confirm that the OB matters.

### Market Structure

High-quality bullish setup:
- Higher timeframe trend or reversal is bullish.
- Price pulls back into higher timeframe bullish OB.
- Lower timeframe shows bullish CHoCH.
- After CHoCH, lower timeframe bullish OB forms.
- Entry is from lower timeframe bullish OB, not immediately from higher timeframe OB.

High-quality bearish setup:
- Higher timeframe trend or reversal is bearish.
- Price pulls back into higher timeframe bearish OB.
- Lower timeframe shows bearish CHoCH.
- After CHoCH, lower timeframe bearish OB forms.
- Entry is from lower timeframe bearish OB.

### Entry Rules To Code

Bullish:
1. Detect HTF bullish OB from FVG.
2. Wait for price to touch HTF bullish OB.
3. On LTF, wait for bullish CHoCH: break above swing high.
4. After CHoCH, detect fresh LTF bullish OB.
5. Buy on retest/touch of LTF bullish OB.

Bearish:
1. Detect HTF bearish OB from FVG.
2. Wait for price to touch HTF bearish OB.
3. On LTF, wait for bearish CHoCH: break below swing low.
4. After CHoCH, detect fresh LTF bearish OB.
5. Sell/short on retest/touch of LTF bearish OB.

### SL Rules

Bullish:
- SL below LTF bullish OB low/wick.
- If OB is very small, use extra buffer.

Bearish:
- SL above LTF bearish OB high/wick.
- If OB is very small, use extra buffer.

### Target Rules

Target 1:
- 1:2 risk reward.
- Partial booking / move SL to breakeven after target 1.

Final target:
- Next important HTF zone.
- Previous swing high/low.
- Opposite order block or liquidity zone.

### Multiple Entries

Multiple lower timeframe OBs can form after confirmation.

Codeable approach:
- Allow multiple setups, but limit risk.
- Use max open trades / max trades per day setting.
- Use reduced position size if pyramiding is enabled.

### Things Not To Code Blindly

- "High quality" visual feel.
- Messy vs clean structure unless converted into filters.
- Important zone selection without a specific rule.
- News/global sentiment inside Pine.

### Pine Approximation Ideas

Inputs:
- HTF timeframe.
- LTF confirmation timeframe.
- Swing length for BOS/CHoCH.
- FVG minimum size.
- OB size filter.
- Wick buffer points.
- Minimum RR.
- Entry mode: OB Top / Mid / Bottom / Touch / Close confirmation.
- Intraday session.
- Max trades per day.
- Alerts: BUY, SELL, SL.

Risk controls:
- No repaint: use confirmed bars only.
- Avoid `lookahead_on`.
- Keep HTF zones only after confirmed HTF candles.
- Strategy entries only after confirmed LTF signal.

### Valid OB Rules

OB must:
- Create inefficiency/FVG.
- Lead to BOS or CHoCH.
- Be unmitigated before trade.

- Entry at beginning of OB.
- SL at far end of OB.

### Quality Filters

Positive OB filters:
- OB move begins after sweeping equal highs/lows.
- Liquidity is grabbed before price reaches the zone.
- OB is fresh and recent.
- OB is aligned with dominant trend.
- Volatility is healthy.
- LTF CHoCH confirms reaction from zone.

Negative OB filters:
- Another valid OB and FVG sit beyond the first OB, making first OB likely inducement.
- Price has spent too much time before retesting old OB.
- Opposite side has taken control by breaking multiple zones.
- Market is range-bound or low liquidity.

LTF confirmation:
- After HTF OB touch, wait for CHoCH.
- If CHoCH leg creates OB + FVG, enter retest of that LTF OB.
- If no FVG forms, use 0.618-0.786 retracement of CHoCH leg as fallback.

### Pine Conversion Notes

Add OB score:
- +1 if liquidity sweep exists before zone.
- +1 if trend aligned.
- +1 if recent/unmitigated.
- -1 if deeper same-direction OB/FVG sits beyond it.
- Skip if score below minimum.

For current VOB strategies:
- If candle touches multiple bullish VOBs, prefer deepest/highest-score zone only.
- Do not open another trade while first related setup is active unless pyramiding is enabled.

Title: Change of Character Simplified - Smart Money Course

Channel: Smart Risk

Core model: valid CHoCH is not a tiny structure break. It should break a meaningful swing and the latest opposite zone after price reacts from a real POI.

### CHoCH Definition

Bearish CHoCH:
- Price breaks and closes below most recent major low.
- Ideally breaks latest demand zone too.

Bullish CHoCH:
- Price breaks and closes above most recent major high.
- Ideally breaks latest supply zone too.

High-quality CHoCH:
- Comes after HTF supply/demand mitigation.
- Breaks meaningful structure by candle close.
- Often breaks two or more internal zones.
- Move is impulsive and leaves inefficiency.

Invalid/weak CHoCH:
- Minor internal break only.
- No prior HTF POI reaction.
- Price merely moves to mitigate another unmitigated zone.

### Pine Conversion Notes

Use two CHoCH strengths:
- Minor: close beyond recent small swing.
- Major: close beyond swing that created previous BOS and/or zone.

For V2:
- Default should require major CHoCH for conservative entries.
- Aggressive mode can allow minor CHoCH but only with liquidity sweep + strong displacement.

## Video 9

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JpQybUVffVI

Title: Best Pullback Trading Strategies In Forex - The Pullback Mastery Guide

Channel: Smart Risk

Core model: first classify pullback quality, then decide whether OB is valid.

### Valid Pullback

Bullish trend:
- Consecutive bullish candles make higher highs.
- A valid pullback requires price to break/sweep the low of a previous candle.
- Candle color does not matter.

Bearish trend:
- Consecutive bearish candles make lower lows.
- A valid pullback requires price to break/sweep the high of a previous candle.

If pullback is invalid:
- Related OB/inducement zone is weak.
- Avoid assuming continuation.

### Pullback Types

Aggressive pullback:
- Fast, large candles against trend.
- Momentum is too strong into the POI.
- Avoid blind entries.

Corrective pullback:
- Slow, smaller candles.
- Creates internal structure.
- Momentum/volatility subsides near POI.
- Better for OB retest entries.

Sweeping pullback:
- Equal lows/highs or trendline liquidity forms before POI.
- Sweep before POI strengthens the trade.

### Pine Conversion Notes

Pullback V2 filters:
- Require valid pullback sweep before OB entry.
- Avoid entry if pullback candles are too large vs ATR.
- Prefer corrective/sweeping pullbacks.

## Unified Strategy Design Notes

The videos repeatedly support the same robust structure:

1. Context

2. Zone
- OB/VOB/FVG must be fresh.

## V2 Build Blueprint

### Pullback V2

Goal:
- Buy options on strong demand/VOB pullbacks, but avoid blind touch buys.

Default conservative rules:
1. Detect bullish VOB/demand that created BOS/FVG.
2. Zone must be fresh or recently formed.
3. Price pulls back into zone.
4. Optional: liquidity sweep before/into zone.
5. Optional: pullback quality must not be aggressive.
6. Wait for bullish confirmation:
   - sweep/reclaim, or
   - LTF major CHoCH, or
   - LTF OB/FVG retest after CHoCH.
7. Buy.
8. SL below active demand zone plus buffer.
9. Target next bearish VOB/supply/liquidity or RR.
10. Trail below latest higher demand/VOB if enabled.

### Breakout V2

Goal:
- Buy option momentum after bearish VOB/supply is broken, but avoid chasing weak breakouts.

Default conservative rules:
1. Detect bearish VOB/supply.
2. Signal candle closes above bearish VOB.
3. Signal candle becomes breakout reference.
4. Entry only when next candle(s) cross signal high, OR after fresh bullish OB/FVG retest.
5. SL below signal low or fresh OB low.
